# Service Performance Intelligence Dashboard

**Tools:** Power BI  
**Category:** Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, Customer Service Analytics  

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze customer service performance, monitor Service Level Agreement (SLA) compliance, and evaluate support team efficiency.

The dashboard consists of four analytical views:

1. Executive Summary
2. Service Level Agreement (SLA)
3. Agent Performance Review
4. Performance Comparison

### Project Objectives

- Monitor customer support volume and operational efficiency.
- Evaluate SLA compliance across ticket categories and geographical regions.
- Analyze agent productivity and customer satisfaction (CSAT).
- Identify performance gaps and operational bottlenecks.
- Support data-driven decision-making through interactive dashboards.

---

## 01. Executive Summary

**Focus:** Customer Support Operations & KPI Monitoring

### Key Insights

- **2,330 total tickets** were recorded, with **1,173 closed tickets**.
- Average first response time was **0.15 hours**.
- Average resolution time was **0.94 days**.
- Product setup (**27.04%**) and pricing and licensing (**22.53%**) accounted for nearly half of all support requests.
- First response SLA compliance reached **86.65%**, compared with **66.39%** for one-day resolution SLA compliance.

### Business Insights

The difference between first response and resolution SLA compliance highlights a potential gap between initial responsiveness and issue resolution efficiency.

Understanding ticket distribution by topic can help support teams prioritize recurring issues and improve resource allocation.

## 02. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Focus:** SLA Compliance & Service Quality

### Key Insights

- First response SLA compliance reached **86.65%**.
- One-day resolution SLA compliance was **66.39%**.
- Training requests recorded the lowest resolution SLA compliance at **57.58%**.
- Bulgaria achieved the highest resolution SLA compliance among the displayed countries at **74.05%**.
- Monthly resolution SLA compliance declined from **76.34% in January** to **60.23% in December**.

### Business Insights

The analysis identifies variations in SLA compliance across ticket categories and geographical regions.

Monitoring these differences can help businesses identify potential service bottlenecks and prioritize operational improvements.

## 03. Agent Performance Review

**Focus:** Agent Productivity, CSAT & Resolution Efficiency

### Key Insights

- **Nicola Wane** handled the highest ticket volume, with **367 tickets**.
- **Connor Danielovitch** achieved the highest CSAT score of **4.07**.
- Despite achieving the highest CSAT score, Connor recorded a resolution SLA compliance rate of **61.67%**.
- SLA performance varied between first-line and second-line support teams.

### Business Insights

Ticket volume alone does not fully represent individual agent performance.

Combining productivity, customer satisfaction, and SLA compliance provides a more balanced approach to evaluating service quality.

This analysis can support targeted coaching, workload management, and performance improvement initiatives.

## 04. Performance Comparison

**Focus:** Period-over-Period Operational Performance

### Key Insights

- First response SLA compliance was **86.65%**, compared with **86.82%** in the previous period.
- Resolution SLA compliance decreased slightly from **66.90% to 66.39%**.
- Average response time remained at **0.02 days** across the displayed periods.
- Average resolution time remained at **0.94 days**.
- Country-level comparisons highlighted differences in SLA compliance and CSAT scores.

### Business Insights

Period-over-period comparisons provide visibility into changes in customer support efficiency and service quality.

These insights help stakeholders monitor performance trends, identify emerging issues, and make informed operational decisions.

## Project Takeaways

This project demonstrates how customer support data can be transformed into actionable business insights through Power BI.

By integrating ticket volume, SLA compliance, customer satisfaction, and agent performance metrics, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view of service operations.

**Key Takeaway:** Effective customer service management requires balancing response efficiency, resolution performance, and customer satisfaction to support continuous operational improvement.
